## 🧠 How I approach engineering

A few principles have stayed with me over the years:

- **Solve the problem, not just the symptom.** Understanding why something fails is often more valuable than making the error disappear.
- **Build for the people who come after you.** Maintainable software, clear documentation, and thoughtful architecture matter.
- **Deployment is part of development.** If I build it, I want to understand how it runs, how it fails, and how it recovers.
- **Good engineering is collaborative.** Some of the best solutions emerge when technical and nontechnical people understand one another.
- **Keep learning.** Experience is useful. Curiosity keeps it from becoming obsolete.
